Need a phone, laptop or scooter repaired on Koh Lanta? These are the places nomads and locals use. Things break fast in the heat and humidity.

## Laptops

We don't currently have a laptop repair place we can vouch for — the previous one (Ek) is permanently closed. If you know a good one, please let us know.

- [TAB Computer](https://www.facebook.com/tabcomputerlanta/) does computer repairs, but we haven't been there yet.
