Most visitors get **30 days visa-free** in Thailand, and you can extend that once by another 30 days. There's no immigration office on Koh Lanta itself, but visa agents on the island can handle the extension for you.

> ⚠️ **Changed in September 2026:** Thailand cut visa-free stays from 60 to 30 days and shortened the list of eligible countries to [60 countries and territories](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40070511). A lot of websites still say 60 days. Check that your country is on the list before you book.

Want to stay longer from the start? Apply for a tourist visa before your trip. It gives you 60 days per entry.

Before you fly, fill out the [Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)](https://tdac.immigration.go.th). It's required for every arrival, within 3 days before you land.

## Is there an immigration office on Koh Lanta?

No. Koh Lanta is part of Krabi province, so extensions and other immigration paperwork go through the [Krabi Immigration Office](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Krabi+Immigration+Office) in Krabi Town on the mainland. Most people skip the trip and use one of the [visa agents on the island](#visa-agents-on-koh-lanta) instead.

## Extending your stay

A 30-day extension costs 1,900 THB, paid in cash. A visa-free stay can only be extended once. Bring:

- Your passport, plus copies of the photo page and your entry stamp
- A passport photo
- The address where you're staying (your accommodation registers you with immigration)
- The TM.7 extension form, which you can fill out at the office

## Visa runs

The closest border crossing is Wang Prachan in Satun, on the Malaysian border. Border-run vans from Krabi do the trip there and back in a day. Leaving and coming back in gets you a fresh 30-day stamp, but visa-exempt visitors can only enter Thailand over land twice per calendar year. Ask your visa agent or accommodation for current options.

## Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)

If you want to stay and work remotely for months, look into the **Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)**. It's a 5-year multiple-entry visa with stays of up to 180 days, which you can extend once by another 180 days. The fee is 10,000 THB and you need 500,000 THB in savings.

Since 31 August 2026 you have to apply at the Thai embassy for the country you're a citizen or legal resident of, and you need a criminal record certificate.
