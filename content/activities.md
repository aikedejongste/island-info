Things to do on Koh Lanta when you're not working or on the beach: a Thai cooking class, go-karting, kayaking through the mangroves and a good massage. For boat trips to the islands, see [island trips and snorkelling](/island-trips-and-snorkelling/).

## Cooking Classes

Koh Lanta has two well-known Thai cooking schools. Both run a day and an evening class, and you eat everything you cook.

### [Time for Lime](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Time+For+Lime+Koh+Lanta)

The original Thai cooking school on Koh Lanta, open since 2002, on Klong Dao Beach in an open-air beachfront setting with a pool. You make curry paste, 2 starters and 2 main courses, then eat what you cooked. Recipes are sent home with you.

All profits go to [Lanta Animal Welfare](https://www.lantaanimalwelfare.com/), which rescues and treats stray animals on the island.

- **Day class:** starts at 11:00 and includes a tour of Lanta Animal Welfare. Children under 13 can join.
- **Evening class:** starts at 17:00 and includes a cocktail class. Adults only.
- Both take about 5 hours.
- **Prices:** 2,300 THB for one person, or 3,600 THB for two people booking together.

Book directly at [timeforlime.net](https://timeforlime.net/contact/). Check their booking calendar for which days classes run.

### [Cooking with Mon](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cooking+with+Mon+Koh+Lanta)

A cooking school in the Long Beach area and one of the best-reviewed on the island (5.0 on Google from more than 850 reviews). Free hotel pick-up and drop-off is included.

- **Day class:** 10:00 to 14:30
- **Evening class:** 16:30 to 21:00
- **Price:** 1,750 THB per person. Children aged 8 to 12 can cook with a parent for 750 THB extra, and under 8 join for free.

Book online at [cookingwithmon.com](http://www.cookingwithmon.com/).

## Go-Karting

### [Lanta E-GoKart](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kUMqmgQyaggVutEP8)

A fun activity for adults and kids. Off season you can usually walk straight in; in high season it's worth checking ahead. After each round you're handed a little baby bottle to feed the koi fish, a cute touch that keeps the kids (and adults) coming back for another lap. Bring your own helmet if you have one.

## Kayaking

### Mangroves

Enter the mangroves, pay the 20b entrance fee, they will try to get you to rent from them or get a guide, ignore that. Then carry on past the toilet, over the bridge, there's a little restaurant on the left that rents yellow/red kayaks for 500b for 2 hours
