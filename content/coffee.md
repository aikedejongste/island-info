Where to find the best coffee on Koh Lanta, and where to buy beans, decaf and equipment to make your own. Looking for a cafe with good WiFi to work from? See the [cafes on the coworking page](/coworking/#cafes-you-can-work-from).

## Cafes

[Slow Bar Caffetaria](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9cmLwVcST9AJKMvg7) - the best coffee on the island. Irregular opening hours, so check before you go.

## Beans

[Slow Bar Caffetaria](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9cmLwVcST9AJKMvg7) - also sells beans

[Oasis Yoga](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Oasis+Yoga+Bungalows+Koh+Lanta) - sells beans in their shop

[Bonnie's Deli](https://maps.app.goo.gl/SSbdDpRoSAuAoVUS8) - sells beans and ground coffee

[Lanta Mart](https://maps.app.goo.gl/grbkRwDV5NPx699K7) - sells beans and ground coffee

## Decaf

You can buy ground decaf coffee online on <https://th.iherb.com?ref=lantanomads.com>.

You can order a decaf americano in [KoHub](https://www.kohub.org).

## Equipment

[Full-Fill Cafe](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Full+Fill+Cafe+Klong+Nin+Koh+Lanta) in Klong Nin sells simple french press cafetières.
