Where to find the best coffee on Koh Lanta, and where to buy beans, decaf and equipment to make your own. Looking for a cafe with good WiFi to work from? See the [cafes on the coworking page](/coworking/#cafes-you-can-work-from).

## Cafes

[Slow Bar Caffetaria](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9cmLwVcST9AJKMvg7) - the best coffee on the island. Irregular opening hours, so check before you go.

[The Wild Brew](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Wild+Brew+Koh+Lanta) - Small coffee shop with a perfect 5.0 on Google from more than 200 reviews (November 2026). Reviewers single out the coffee beans and the owner, who takes time for every customer.

[Mem's Coffee Corner](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mem%27s+Coffee+Corner+Koh+Lanta) - On the road to Lanta Animal Welfare, and one of the best-reviewed cafes on the island (4.9 from more than 900 reviews). Good coffee and breakfast.

[Peak Café](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Peak+Cafe+Koh+Lanta) - Popular for its iced coffee drinks, with a big menu (4.7 from more than 850 reviews).

## Decaf

You can buy ground decaf coffee online on <https://th.iherb.com?ref=lantanomads.com>.

You can order a decaf americano in [KoHub](https://www.kohub.org).

## Equipment

[FULL-FILL Café](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ChZKzrBQ98fPAmco6), a vegetarian cafe and refill store in Klong Nin, sells simple french press cafetières.
