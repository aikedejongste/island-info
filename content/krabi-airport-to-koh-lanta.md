Krabi Airport is the closest airport to Koh Lanta, about 75 km away. A private car takes around 2 hours and a shared minivan 2 to 2.5 hours, plus whatever time you spend waiting for the car ferry. Koh Lanta has no airport of its own.

## Shared minivan

The cheapest way to the island. Expect to pay about 400-550 THB per person, with drop-off at your hotel. You can buy a ticket at the transport counters in the arrivals hall or book online in advance (12Go and similar sites), which is usually a bit cheaper than buying at the counter.

Vans leave roughly every hour during the day. The last ones go somewhere between 18:30 and 20:30, depending on the company. Vans can wait to fill up before leaving, so don't plan anything tight on the island for the first few hours.

## Private transfer

About 2,100-3,300 THB for the car, more for a bigger van. The driver waits for you at arrivals and leaves when you do. Worth it if you're with a group, have a lot of luggage or land late.

## Can I take a Grab from Krabi Airport to Koh Lanta?

Grab picks up at Krabi Airport (International Arrivals, gate 17), but drivers don't reliably accept a trip all the way to Koh Lanta. Check the price in the app and have a backup. Once you're on the island, [Grab doesn't work at all](/grab/).

## The car ferry

There's no road bridge from the mainland yet. Every car and minivan crosses on a small car ferry from Hua Hin pier to Koh Lanta Noi, then drives over the Siri Lanta Bridge to Koh Lanta Yai, where everything is.

- The ferry runs from about 6am to 10pm. It doesn't run at night.
- There's no timetable. It leaves when it's full, and the crossing takes 10-20 minutes.
- Passengers get out of the car and buy their own ticket at the booth before the pier. Expect about 20 THB per person and 60-100 THB per car (foreigners pay more than Thais). The driver takes the car across.
- In high season, especially December and January afternoons, the queue can add one to two hours or more.

### Landing late?

The last shared minivans leave the airport around 18:30-20:30. If you land after about 20:00, book a private transfer and check with the driver that you'll make it to the pier before the ferry stops around 10pm. Otherwise you'll need a night in Krabi.

## Will there be a bridge?

Yes, but not soon. The contract for a bridge from the mainland to Koh Lanta Noi was signed in August 2026, with a target opening in 2029. The project has slipped before.
