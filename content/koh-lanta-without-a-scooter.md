You can do Koh Lanta without a scooter, as long as you stay somewhere you can walk to the beach, food and shops, and use taxis or tuk-tuks for everything else. The island as a whole isn't walkable: it's about 30 km from north to south, and there's no public transport.

## Where to stay

Pick a place on or near the beach road at Long Beach, Klong Dao or around Saladan. There you'll have restaurants, cafes, shops and tuk-tuks within walking distance. Two of the coworking spaces, [KoHub and Monkey Business](/coworking/), are in the Long Beach area too.

Avoid places at the end of a long side road. Even a nice bungalow gets tiring when every coffee means calling a ride. See [where to stay](/accommodation/) for places in the Long Beach area.

## Taxis

There's no meter and no app: you message a driver on WhatsApp, agree on the price and they come to you. Expect 200-500 THB depending on distance, and always agree on the fare before you get in. Save a few numbers when you arrive. The [Grab page](/grab/#how-people-actually-get-around) lists a few drivers.

## Tuk-tuks

Tuk-tuks wait around Saladan and the busier beaches. They're easier to find than taxis and handy for short hops or a night out, but they usually cost more per trip than calling a driver. Agree on the price first.

## E-bikes and bicycles

- [Koh Lanta Bike Rentals](https://maps.app.goo.gl/uipWxpP8qxHMzeKG8) rents e-bikes, a good option for shorter trips if you don't have a licence or don't want to ride a scooter.
- Regular bicycles are rented around Long Beach and Klong Dao for roughly 100-200 THB a day. The north of the island is flatter, the south gets hilly, and the heat makes long rides hard work.

## Renting a car

Car rental is less common than scooters, but several agencies in Saladan rent cars, like [Happytime](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Happytime+Motorbike+Car+Rental+Koh+Lanta) and [SBD](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=SBD+Motorbike+and+Car+Rental+Koh+Lanta), both well reviewed on Google Maps. It's the easiest option for families or if you'd rather not ride. You need your driving licence and an international driving permit, just like for a scooter (see the [driving tips](/transportation/#driving-tips)).

## Day trips without driving

- Book [island trips](/island-trips-and-snorkelling/) and tours that include hotel pick-up. Many do.
- For a day exploring the south of the island or the Old Town, ask your accommodation to arrange a driver for the day.
- [Grab and Bolt don't work on Koh Lanta](/grab/), so don't count on an app.
