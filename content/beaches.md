Honestly, you can't go wrong with beaches on Lanta — the west coast has many beaches. What follows is a guide to how they differ so you can pick the right one for the day.

{{< beach title="Khlong Dao Beach" img="/images/beaches/khlong-dao.jpg" alt="Khlong Dao Beach, Koh Lanta" >}}
[Khlong Dao Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ypdRjmCqeoo7PXbB6) - The northernmost of the main beaches, closest to Saladan. A long, wide stretch of gently shelving sand that's great for swimming and families, with plenty of restaurants, bars and resorts along the back. Recommended bar: [Majestic bar](https://maps.app.goo.gl/2LrGJpFV3f4jX27b6)
{{< /beach >}}

{{< beach title="Long Beach (Phra Ae)" img="/images/beaches/long-beach.jpg" alt="Long Beach (Phra Ae), Koh Lanta" >}}
[Long Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/wmKSE4Zy3m6RZaDB9) - Just south of Khlong Dao and, as the name suggests, very long. A popular base with lots of accommodation, beach bars and sunset spots, but still spacious enough to find a quiet stretch. [Lym's Bar](https://maps.app.goo.gl/44cgz6KnatisbBuY8) is where most nomads go for sunset drinks.
{{< /beach >}}

{{< beach title="Klong Khong" img="/images/beaches/klong-khong.jpg" alt="Klong Khong Beach at low tide, Koh Lanta" >}}
[Klong Khong](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Klong+Khong+Beach+Koh+Lanta) - South of Long Beach, with a string of beach bars like [Freedom Bar](/drinks/) and [Sound Shack](/drinks/). There are rocks along the waterline, so swimming is awkward at low tide. Better for a drink and the sunset than for a swim.
{{< /beach >}}

{{< beach title="Klong Nin" img="/images/beaches/klong-nin.jpg" alt="Klong Nin Beach, Koh Lanta" >}}
[Klong Nin](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Klong+Nin+Beach+Koh+Lanta) - A sandy beach further south and one of the better ones for swimming, although it gets narrow at high tide. On Sundays, [Horizon Bar](/parties/#sunday) is a good sunset spot.
{{< /beach >}}

{{< beach title="Nui Bay" img="/images/beaches/nui-bay.jpg" alt="Nui Bay, Koh Lanta" >}}
[Nui Bay](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HRowPbfxxP65j6Zr6) - A small, beautiful cove reached by some steep steps. There are no real facilities here — sometimes there are monkeys, and sometimes someone selling drinks, but that's about it. Come for the scenery and seclusion rather than amenities, keep an eye on your belongings around the monkeys, and bring what you need with you.
{{< /beach >}}

{{< beach title="Beautiful Beach & Secret Beach" >}}
[Beautiful Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WhL96Bcbb1k8D7Bw9) - A gorgeous cove further south. Since 2026 there is a parking fee, and a gate that is closed at night, so plan around that if you're heading out for sunset.

[Secret Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vhjYgfoUkVegcxiR6) - Tucked in right next to Beautiful Beach. To reach it you park at Beautiful Beach and walk over — there's no separate access — so the same parking fee and night gate apply. No facilities and no bars.
{{< /beach >}}

{{< beach title="Kantiang Bay" img="/images/beaches/kantiang-bay.jpg" alt="Kantiang Bay, Koh Lanta" >}}
[Kantiang Bay](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Xs8GnHr49sPLFLNu9) - A stunning bay much further south, backed by jungle hills and well worth the drive. While you're there, don't miss [Why Not Bar](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fTYmGHkDNNcF3U3R6), a really nice bar right by the beach. They often have a DJ after 17h and they also serve food from a nearby restaurant.
{{< /beach >}}

{{< beach title="Klong Jak" >}}
[Klong Jak](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Klong+Jak+Beach+Koh+Lanta) - Further south again, past Kantiang Bay. A beautiful bay that's good for swimming.
{{< /beach >}}

## When is it safe to swim?

From November to April the sea on the west coast is usually calm and every beach here is swimmable. From May to October, waves and currents get much stronger and the southern beaches often fly red flags. In those months, stick to the northern beaches like Khlong Dao and Long Beach, and stay out of the water when the red flag is up.
