The best time to visit Koh Lanta is December to March. It's dry and sunny, the sea is calm, and everything is open. November and April are good shoulder months. From May to October it's rainy season, and most of the island closes.

## Dry season: November to April

From November the wind comes from the northeast. All of Koh Lanta's beaches face west, so they're sheltered: the sea is calm and clear, and good for swimming.

- December to March is the best time. January and February are the driest months, with only two or three rainy days.
- December to February is also the busiest and most expensive time, especially around Christmas and New Year. KoHub's coliving, for example, costs 89,500 THB a month in January and February against 49,500 in November and April. Book by October or November (see the [booking tips](/accommodation/#tips-for-booking)).
- November still has some rain, and places reopen through the month. [KoHub](/coworking/) opens on 1 November.
- April is the hottest month, and the rain starts to come back. It's quieter and cheaper than the peak months.

## Rainy season: May to October

From May the wind turns to the southwest, and the west-facing beaches catch the waves and storms straight from the Indian Ocean. August to October is the wettest stretch. There are sunny patches, but most days are wet and overcast.

What that means on the island:

- Most of the island closes. KoHub estimates about 80% of businesses shut for the season, and [KoHub itself](https://kohub.org/seasons) is closed from May to October. [Monkey Business](/coworking/) is the coworking space that stays open year-round.
- The sea gets rough. Waves and currents get strong, the beaches collect trash from the ocean, and red flags go up, especially in the south. See [when it's safe to swim](/beaches/#when-is-it-safe-to-swim).
- Snorkelling and island trips stop, and the national park closes Koh Rok and Koh Haa every year. The exact dates change from year to year, so check with your tour operator.
- Boats from Phuket and Phi Phi keep running all year, just less often, speedboats from Ao Nang pause for part of the season, and most island-hopping routes stop. See [getting to Koh Lanta](/getting-here/).
- The places that stay open are often 30-50% cheaper than in high season.

Rainy season can also bring jellyfish. In June 2026 the national park [closed Koh Ngai, Koh Haa and the beach at the park headquarters](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40067300) for a while after Portuguese man o' war washed up.

## Where to go in low season

Low season works if all you need is a quiet base with good internet: Monkey Business is open and accommodation is cheap. For beaches and island trips it's the wrong time, and many nomads leave for the season. Places the [KoHub community](https://kohub.org/seasons) suggests include Chiang Mai, the islands in the Gulf of Thailand (Koh Phangan, Koh Samui, Koh Tao), Bali and Lisbon.
