About Lanta Nomads

Last updated November 22, 2026

Hi, I’m Jacob. I’m a digital nomad, and since 2015 I’ve been coming to Koh Lanta most winters. I work remotely from Kohub. That’s also what brought me to the island in the first place.

Over the years I’ve collected a lot of tips and suggestions: where to work, where to eat, how to get around and what to do for fun. For years I kept these in a Google Keep document and in 2026 I’ve finally put them online so you can benefit too.

New to the island? Start with getting to Koh Lanta and where to work.

Enjoy,

Jacob
